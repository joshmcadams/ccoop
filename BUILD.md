# Construction Sequence & Instructions

Because this coop is a "pole barn" structure rather than standard stick-framing, the construction sequence prioritizes locking the main structural skeleton (poles and roof) together before adding infill walls.

## Phase 1: Site Prep & Posts
1. **Layout:** Mark out the 6 post holes. The grid is exactly 2180mm wide by 1580mm deep (center-to-center).
2. **Digging:** Dig holes to roughly 600mm depth.
3. **Setting Posts:** Set the 6 H4 round posts (115mm-120mm diameter). Use the 3.6m posts for the front row and the 3.0m posts for the back row. Ensure they are perfectly plumb. 
4. **Locking In:** Backfill with concrete (Quickset).

## Phase 2: Floor Framing (Bearers & Joists)
1. **Level Line:** Measure and mark a level line across all posts. The bottom of the floor framing will sit here.
2. **Bearers:** Take two uncut 3.0m lengths of 150x50mm timber. Bolt them horizontally to the **outside** faces of the front and rear posts. Center them so they overhang the posts evenly on the left and right (creating a 300mm cantilever).
3. **Hardware Orientation:** Use two M12x200mm carriage bolts per post. Drive them from the **outside in** (smooth head on the exposed outside of the bearer, sharp threads and nut safely hidden inside the post under the floorboards).
4. **Joists:** Cut eight 150x50mm joists to 1.70m length. Attach them running front-to-back between the bearers using galvanized steel joist hangers. Space them symmetrically at 470mm centers to perfectly dodge the carriage bolts protruding from the center posts. The two outermost joists sit perfectly flush against the outside face of the left and right posts, forming a solid 2.4x1.8m box.

## Phase 3: Plywood Floor (Zero Waste)
1. **Main Floor (1.5 Sheets):** Take a standard 2400x1200x17mm plywood sheet. Cut three 130x170mm notches into the back edge to slide it around the three back posts. Fasten it down. Take a second sheet, rip it in half (2400x600mm), cut three notches in its front edge, and slide it around the front posts.
2. **Cantilever Floors:** Take the remaining 2400x600mm offcut. Rip it in half lengthwise (to get two 2400x300mm strips) and cross-cut them down to 1800mm. Drop these two 1800x300mm pieces onto the left and right cantilevers to cap them off. You have now floored the entire footprint and cantilevers using exactly 2 sheets of plywood!

## Phase 4: Roof Framing (The Skeleton)
1. **Roof Bearers:** Cut the tops of the front and back posts to the desired roof angle. Bolt a heavy timber bearer across the front three posts, and another across the back three posts.
2. **Rafters:** Run rafters spanning from the front roof bearer down to the back roof bearer.
3. **Purlins:** Run purlins horizontally across the rafters to support the roofing iron.
*(Locking the roof together braces the entire pole structure, making it rigid enough to handle wall framing).*

## Phase 5: Infill Wall Framing
1. **Bottom Plates:** Lay 90x45mm timber flat on the plywood floor, spanning between the posts. The outside edge of this wall plate should stack perfectly flush with the edge of the plywood and the outside face of the floor bearer below it.
2. **Studs & Top Plates:** Frame vertical studs up to the roofline, framing out gaps for the human door, chicken pop-door, and nesting box access.

## Phase 6: Nesting Boxes & Cladding
1. Build the nesting box framing out onto the 300mm cantilevered floor platform, tying it securely into the wall framing.
2. Wrap the entire structure in your chosen cladding (e.g., plywood or corrugated iron), letting the cladding drop straight down past the floor framing like a skirt to seal out the weather.
3. Attach the roof iron, doors, and ramp.
