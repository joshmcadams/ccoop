# Coop Design Decisions

## Dimensions & Layout
* **Footprint:** 2400mm x 1800mm (8x6 ft).
* **Rationale:** This is optimized for standard NZ plywood sheets (2400x1200mm). The floor will use exactly 1.5 sheets (one full sheet + one ripped in half lengthways) with zero offcuts.

## Foundation & Posts
* **Style:** Pole-barn style on sloped ground.
* **Posts:** 6x H4 Treated Round Fence Posts (115mm/120mm diameter).
* **Depths/Lengths:** Buried 600mm. Front row needs 3.6m posts (trimmed to ~3.44m). Back row needs 3.0m posts (trimmed to ~2.8m).
* **Placement:** The posts are placed *outside* the 2400x1800 footprint. Center-to-center width is 2520mm, and center-to-center depth is 1920mm. This allows the floor framing to sit perfectly inside the posts without intersection.

## Floor Framing
* **Bearers:** Two 150x50mm timbers running the 2.52m width, bolted to the *inside* faces of the posts.
* **Joists:** Five 150x50mm timbers (1.7m long) spaced evenly (~587mm on center). They are hung *flush* between the front and back bearers using joist hangers, acting as a flat perimeter for the plywood.

## Roof & Nesting
* **Roof:** Sloped single-plane (skillion) roof to easily shed rain downhill.
* **Nesting Boxes:** Exterior protruding boxes to save interior floor space. Built with an overhanging, sloped lid (angled -15 degrees away from the coop) to prevent water pooling.
* **Clean-out:** A full-width hatch on the rear (downhill side) allows sweeping soiled bedding directly into a wheelbarrow.
